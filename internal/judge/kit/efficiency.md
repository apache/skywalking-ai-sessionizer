## Task: what each piece of the agent's own work was

The targets are listed below, each with its talks. Label the work of each target so asz can price it. "This run" says whether the sub-tasks are judged in a stage of their own, and so whether your labels cover them. asz knows what every talk cost, and it adds up the cost of each label. You decide only what the work was.

"This run" lists the talks this call labels. Give one label per talk of a target, for those talks only. When a talk changed course, label each part instead: set `from` to the id of the first step of the part, and the part runs to the next part or the end of the talk. Leave `from` empty for a whole talk. Work before the first part is not reviewed unless a label of the whole talk covers it, so start the first part at the talk's start, or give a label of the whole talk as well.

Use the labels of work given above. Judge earlier work against the target as it stood then, not as it was changed later. "This run" lists the sub-tasks the sub-task stage found misled the agent: the agent acted on a report that was not true. Label the work that followed from such a report `misled_by_subtask`, from the step that acted on it, with the sub-task's id in `subtask`; leave `subtask` empty for every other label. A `misled_by_subtask` label names one of those sub-tasks and begins after its report came back, or it is left out: in the talk the report came back to, with the result of the step that started it or with the runtime notice that it ended, set `from` to the step that acted on it. The `subtasks` tool gives each sub-task's decision. Every label cites at least one step of the talk it labels, the one you read to decide it; for any label other than the first three, the step that shows why. A talk id is not a step. A label with no step of its talk is left out, and its cost counts as not reviewed.

Read where the cost is. `talks` gives every talk's cost with its sub-tasks. Look first at the costliest talks, and at talks with failed tools or many repeated steps. Then open their outline with `talk`. You may leave cheap talks without a label: asz marks them `not_reviewed`, and says how much cost was not reviewed. Put one sentence on why in `note`.
